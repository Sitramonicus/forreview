SYNTHETIC INPUTS 01

Monthly contribution preview

2026 RULESET · CHANGEABLE

Enter fictional values. The statutory bases are separate on purpose: SSS compensation, PhilHealth monthly basic salary, Pag-IBIG Fund Salary, taxable compensation, and cash pay can differ. The sample starts each at ₱25,000.

No calculation run yet. Values are not saved.
Open source notes and assumptions

SSS: 2025 schedule remains the latest published contribution table found for the 2026 preview; employee 5% and employer 10% apply to MSC up to ₱35,000, with EC ₱10/₱30 paid by employer. SSS contribution schedule

PhilHealth: 5% of monthly basic salary with ₱10,000 floor/₱100,000 ceiling, split equally. Commission, overtime and allowances are excluded from the MBS base. PA2025-0002

Pag-IBIG: MFS ceiling ₱10,000; employee 1% at ₱1,500 and below or 2% above; employer 2%. Circular No. 460

BIR: uses the published monthly withholding table from Annex E to RR 11-2018 (effective 2023 onward), not the annualized method used by one third-party library. The monthly/annualized example differs by ₱0.05. BIR Annex E

All bases are typed separately by the user, and centavo rounding is a provisional half-up rule. No employee status, eligibility, semi-monthly deduction split, non-cash benefits, 13th-month annualisation, or tax adjustment is inferred.
