Can't get in?

Locked out or forgot your password

HR or the System Administrator can restore the shared initial password. Ask them in person or through your usual IT support channel. Your account holder can change the password from My account at any time.

"That page does not exist"

The address was mistyped or the page moved. Start again from the sign-in page.

A figure on your payslip looks wrong

Tell Payroll before the cutoff closes. Once a payslip is released it is not edited; a correction is issued instead, so the record of what was paid stays accurate.

Your details are out of date

HR maintains the employee file. Ask them to update your position, department, branch or supervisor, and the change appears here.
