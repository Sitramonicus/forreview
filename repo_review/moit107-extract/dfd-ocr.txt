A data flow diagram is an effective tool for simulating and understanding the movement of
data inside a system or process. It plays a crucial role in the analysis, design, and
documentation of systems, assisting in ensuring the swift and correct processing and sharing
of data. Your task this week is to create a data flow diagram for the MotorPH Payroll System
Database. To carry out this activity, follow the steps below:

Instructions:
1. Access the following business information:
a. Business Requirement Specification Document for Payroll System

b. MotorPH Existing Business Processes - Payroll
2. Read and examine the information found within the materials.

3. In DrawlO, make a data flow diagram that illustrates how the data flows in the
MotorPH payroll system using the Gene & Sarson notation style.
a. Create the data flow diagram at least until Level 2.
4. Once completed, insert a snapshot of the diagram, fill in the required information in the
template provided below, and delete this section prior to submission.
