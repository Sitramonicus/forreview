1.​   Database Overview
      The MotorPH Payroll System database is built in a way to allow users to automate,
      organise, and streamline MotorPH payroll processes systematically to provide the
      prerogative of having minimal struggle in handling the payroll operations so that users
      may allocate efforts unto more substantially demanding tasks. Through the
      centralisation of most payroll-related data into a singular, secure, and scalable
      repository, the system facilitates rapid data retrieval and processing, which becomes
      indispensable for timely salary computations, tax management, and adherence to legal
      contributions and compliance. Thus, for this instance, we deliver an expectation that
      this database will not just bring about changes in improvement with data derivation
      and the alleviation of unnecessary exertion but also an effective means through which
      future growth of the organisation may be facilitated at incorporation through adaptive
      mechanisms for data expansion.
         1.​ Employee Management – Captures, updates, and maintains employee records,
             including employment status, department affiliation, salary structure, and
             personal information; this module serves as the primary reference for all
             modules, with nearly every module being eventually tied back to this module’s
             unique identifier.
         2.​ Payroll Processing – Automates payroll computation, taking into account
             attendance records, salary adjustments, overtime hours, tax deductions, and
             government-mandated contributions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, Withholding
             Tax). The system ensures accuracy in salary computation as it does so
             simultaneously       with   the   updating     data,   and furthermore, provides a
             semi-monthly payslip for each employee and a monthly summary for each
             payroll processing cycle.
         3.​ Attendance and Leave Tracking – Integrates with an employee attendance
             logging system to automatically fetch clock-in and clock-out data, whilst
             recording and cataloguing leave applications, ensuring that payroll calculations
             incorporate approved absences, accounts for your active work presence during
             holidays and rest days, and determine applicable leave benefits.
         4.​ Government Contributions and Compliance – Calculates and maintains records
             of   statutory   government-related          organisational   contributions,   including
             employer-employee deductions for government agencies, as mentioned prior.
             The system automatically generates tax reports and government contribution
             summaries to assist in compliance with regulatory bodies.
         5.​ Security and Access Control - Among the features are that the system ensures
             role-based access control (RBAC) for different user levels, restricting


                                                                                                   3
modifications to authorised personnel only, with System administrators, HR
personnel, payroll staff, and employees having hierarchical/tiered access to
sensitive information, reducing the risk of unauthorised modifications, and
should such modifications exist, the medium account responsible for this act is
easily traceable through audit logs. Furthermore, access to an employee’s
personally identifiable information is barred, needing the approval of two
authorised personnel, and only their corresponding contribution values are
normally visible and accessible.
