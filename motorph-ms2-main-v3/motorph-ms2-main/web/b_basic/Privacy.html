PRIVACY

What this system holds about you

What is recorded

Your employee number, name, position, department, branch, immediate supervisor and employment status, all taken from the employee file HR maintains. Time-in and time-out records you or your supervisor enter. Leave, overtime and ticket requests you submit. Pay figures derived from those records. Sign-in attempts and the actions taken with an account, for the audit trail.

What is not recorded

No home address, no personal phone number, no email address, and no payment details are displayed in the application. Passwords are stored only as Argon2id hashes; nobody can read them back, including the people who run the system.

Who can see it

You see your own record. Your supervisor sees the people in their reporting line. HR and Payroll see what their roles need. The Chief Executive, Finance and Operations officers read company-wide figures. The System Administrator administers accounts and does not read salaries. Every one of those limits is enforced by the server, not by hiding a button.

How long it is kept

Attendance and payslip records are kept for the retention period the company sets, which the owner must confirm before this goes into production. Sign-in sessions last eight hours at most and are deleted when they expire.

Asking for a copy or a correction

Ask HR for a copy of what the system holds, or to correct it. If a correction changes pay, Payroll issues an adjustment rather than editing a paid payslip, so the record of what was actually paid stays intact.

Before this is used for real

The company must name a Data Protection Officer, confirm the retention period, and supply a privacy contact address. Those three details are deliberately left blank here rather than invented.
